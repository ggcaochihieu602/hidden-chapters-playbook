# AI thumbnail prompt rules — Hidden Chapters

User instruction, 2026-10-04. Apply to every new thumbnail prompt. Preserve video-specific title and existing 4K badge/layout requirements.

Every prompt begins verbatim with these three lines:

Sony A7R IV, 35mm f/1.8, environmental portrait
Leica Summilux close-up, natural soft diffusion
IMG_9854.CR2, RAW.16bit.ACEScg

Then describe, in order:
1. Specific subject and action: gaze, posture, task; avoid default camera-facing poses.
2. Setting: grounded in script and verified locality references; invented illustrative scenes must not imply actual interview identities, exact business locations or observed events.
3. Photographic style: natural skin texture, restrained saturation, ordinary contrast, minimal retouching. Choose one primary framing to resolve environmental portrait versus close-up.
4. Source and direction of light: plausible practical/daylight sources; preserve natural shadows and uneven brightness.
5. Camera and composition: select 16:9, specify photographer position, distance, angle, focus and crop; keep subject clear at thumbnail size and reserve title/badge areas.
6. Materials, colours and emotion: include only two or three justified imperfections such as creased clothes or used objects. Avoid excessive dust, scratches or grain.

Keep actions, lighting, objects, colours and camera approach consistent. Avoid overly polished stock-photo aesthetics and exaggerated beauty language such as flawless, HDR, masterpiece or 8K.
The first three lines are aesthetic instructions, not verified metadata or a promise of RAW/16-bit/ACEScg output.

When revising a prompt after an image is generated, preserve the original used prompt in the working workspace and mark the revised prompt as intended for the next generation. Do not claim an existing image was generated with the revised prompt.
