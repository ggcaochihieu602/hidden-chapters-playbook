Image handling (ver 32): only the aspect ratio must be right; exact detail is NOT required. (1) Images not matching the timeline ratio (default 16:9): use AI (Codex built-in image_gen) to expand or fully REGENERATE a new 16:9 image using the original as reference. Changing real details is allowed; no factual match with the original is required. Images only need to be reasonably plausible: correct ratio, same subject/mood as the clause, natural overall look, no black bars/colour bands/blur padding, no stretching. Do NOT fix minor defects: fake/gibberish text on books, signs, clothing or symbols, printed figures on clothes, fingers, patterns, background props. Regenerate only for major defects visible at a glance at playback speed (wrong ratio, bars, stretching, clearly off-topic, the main subject clearly deformed), at most once; if the second try still has minor flaws, use it. Images within ~5% of 16:9 may simply be cropped. (2) Low-quality images only (blurry, noisy, compression artefacts, too small: guide = short side under 720 px or visibly soft at 1080p): enhance with AI, in the same pass as (1) when also off-ratio. 4K is NOT required; the AI tool's default output size (e.g. ~1672x941) is acceptable and Resolve scales it on the 4K timeline. (3) Images already 16:9 and sharp enough are used directly with no AI step.
