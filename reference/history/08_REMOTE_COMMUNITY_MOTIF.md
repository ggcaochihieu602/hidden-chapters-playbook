## Preserve the storytelling function

- Remote, geographically exceptional places with recognisable everyday life at the centre.
- Title: How People Live in [PLACE] | [specific geographic paradox or human question] | 4K Travel. The delivery suffix depends on the final master.
- Begin with a concrete visual contradiction, then make three promises the film can pay off.
- Follow practical causality: where the town sits -> connections -> food -> shelter/heat -> wilderness boundary -> history -> present work/knowledge -> community -> a larger consequence -> an ending that revisits the opening.
- Retain sensory, dramatic, confident language and a sense of discovery. Do not flatten an approved script into a cautious technical report or silently rewrite it while producing VO.
- Scale from landscape to tangible objects and ordinary actions, then back out. Use warm human observations, suspense and contrasting conditions.
- Around 30 minutes, approximately 3,700–4,000 English words as a starting point; recorded voice determines the actual duration.
- New destinations can change the chapter count and sequence where their own story requires it. Do not force a seed vault, polar night, disaster or mining history onto another location.
